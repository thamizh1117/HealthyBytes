How it works

  • BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, one of the most accurate and widely used formulas.
  • TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) adjusts your BMR based on how active you are.
  • Calorie target is your TDEE shifted up or down depending on whether you want to lose, maintain, or gain weight.
  • Macros (protein, carbs, fat) are split according to your goal to support your body's needs.
